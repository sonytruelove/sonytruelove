### English

**Fullstack developer and AI engineer**, around 3 years of commercial experience, based in Irkutsk, Russia. I work across the stack with a focus on backend and applied AI (RAG, search, NLP), in an AI-native workflow with Claude Code.

**Open to work**: full-time or project-based, on-site in Irkutsk or remote.

**Latest project: [Baikal EcoAssistant](https://github.com/sonytruelove/EcoAssistant)** (2025 to 2026)
Owned the search and answer backend of a 15+ agent educational AI assistant on the flora and fauna of Lake Baikal (partner: Baikal Museum SB RAS; support: Sber). Built on Clean Architecture: hybrid semantic and geo search (PostgreSQL + pgvector + PostGIS), RAG pipeline on LangChain (FAISS, bge-m3, reranking), automated annotation via GigaChat and Label Studio, rule-based NLP on Yargy. Cut the heaviest geo queries from ~50s to ~12s on a cold cache (about 76%) and raised RAG accuracy by around 20% through model selection.

Also: 3+ years in VK Testers with 3000+ reports and around 300 confirmed vulnerabilities, including VK ID regression testing.
